============================================================
1. PRODUCT DEFINITION
============================================================

Product name:

RetroDrive

Product concept:

RetroDrive is a vehicle-adaptive auxiliary instrument, diagnostics,
navigation, condition-monitoring and expedition display intended especially
for enthusiast, classic, youngtimer and off-road vehicles.

It combines modern electronics with interfaces visually appropriate to the
vehicle era.

The device is NOT simply an OBD display.

Its architecture must support multiple vehicle physical layers and multiple
vehicle-specific software profiles.

Initial prioritized targets:

A. Generic CAN OBD-II vehicle
B. 1998 Rover Mini MPi / MEMS 2J
C. Toyota Altezza SXE10 profile scaffold
D. future Subaru GC8 SSM support
E. future Mitsubishi MUT support
F. future Defender/Pajero/Land Cruiser profiles

Only claim a vehicle as VERIFIED after actual hardware testing.

Each vehicle profile must therefore contain:

verification_status:
    unverified
    bench_verified
    vehicle_verified

Do not claim universal vehicle compatibility.

Use the phrase:

"multi-protocol expandable vehicle interface"

rather than:

"works with every car."

============================================================
4. VEHICLE CONNECTOR PHILOSOPHY
============================================================

RetroDrive must support interchangeable vehicle harnesses.

Primary harness:

J1962 / OBD-II 16-pin male connector.

Bring ALL 16 vehicle pins into the custom PCB.

Do NOT connect all 16 pins directly to the ESP32.

Classify them as follows:

Pin 4:
chassis ground

Pin 5:
signal ground

Pin 16:
vehicle battery

Pins 6 / 14:
CAN-H / CAN-L

Pin 7:
K-Line

Pin 15:
legacy L-Line / manufacturer-specific depending vehicle

Pins 2 / 10:
SAE J1850 provision

Pins:
1, 3, 8, 9, 11, 12, 13

manufacturer-defined / expansion.

Manufacturer-specific pins MUST NOT be directly driven by the MCU.

They must go through:

ESD protection
series protection
test point
optional 0-ohm link / jumper
future interface connection

PCB Revision A should use configurable jumpers / 0-ohm links rather than
an elaborate electronic switching matrix.

Provide expansion capability for a future electronic vehicle-pin matrix.

7. CAN INTERFACE
============================================================

The selected Waveshare already contains a TJA1051-family CAN transceiver.

Use the onboard CAN transceiver unless a documented reason requires another.

Vehicle:

OBD pin 6 -> protected CAN-H -> Waveshare CAN-H
OBD pin 14 -> protected CAN-L -> Waveshare CAN-L

Add automotive CAN ESD protection close to the vehicle connector.

Provide optional common-mode choke footprint if justified.

CAN pair:

route together
minimize stubs
avoid noisy switching power area
use manufacturer PCB stackup for impedance calculation.

IMPORTANT:

Do NOT permanently add another 120-ohm termination resistor for vehicle use.

Vehicle CAN networks are already terminated at the bus ends.

Provide an optional:

120-ohm termination
+
jumper / solder bridge

ONLY for standalone bench testing.

Default manufacturing configuration:
termination NOT populated / disabled.

============================================================
45. SAFETY RULES
============================================================

Normal RetroDrive operation is READ-ONLY.

No ECU programming.

No calibration writing.

No immobilizer commands.

No airbag actuation.

No ABS actuation.

No injector/ignition actuation while driving.

Any future service commands must exist in a physically stationary,
explicit service mode and require deliberate confirmation.

The display must never interfere with ECU traffic more than required.

Do not continuously broadcast unnecessary diagnostic requests.

Implement bus-load limits.

53. CRITICAL ENGINEERING BEHAVIOUR
============================================================

Do not hallucinate component specifications.

Do not hallucinate vehicle protocols.

Do not invent ECU commands.

Do not invent connector pinouts.

Do not invent PCB dimensions.

Do not invent part availability.

Do not invent measurement results.

Whenever information is uncertain, mark:

ASSUMPTION
NEEDS VERIFICATION
DNP
UNVERIFIED VEHICLE PROFILE

as appropriate.

Use primary manufacturer datasheets wherever possible.

For vehicle-specific information, maintain source references.

Prefer a functioning, testable implementation over unnecessary abstraction.

Preserve the existing RetroDrive design direction.

The end product must feel like a coherent automotive product, not an
engineering-development screen.
